How deals get on the board

A DFW price, or it doesn’t post

Every flight + hotel deal shows a price for flying out of DFW, or the seller’s Dallas air add-on for an international trip. International flights from Dallas leave from DFW. Headline prices from other cities don’t count.

Per person, two sharing

Package and cruise prices are per traveler for two adults in one room or cabin, as the seller advertised them the day we checked. What’s included varies and is listed on each deal.

Cruise fares are the lead-in

Cruise prices are the cheapest cabin the seller lists for that sailing. Taxes, port fees and gratuities are extra unless the deal says otherwise.

Checked every morning

A daily check re-confirms prices and retires deals that sold out or expired, and a full search for new deals runs every Monday. Deals past their book-by or sailing dates hide themselves right away.

Galveston is the home port

Every cruise sails round trip from Galveston, about 285 miles down I-45 from Dallas. Parking at the cruise terminal is extra.

Book with the seller

Links open the seller’s own page. Prices move daily, so confirm the final price there before you pay.
